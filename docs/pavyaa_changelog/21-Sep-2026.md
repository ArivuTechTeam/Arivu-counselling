## Document figures (work-pavyaa)

Attaches a real figure from the source PDF (for example a "find the angle"
geometry diagram) to the generated questions that need it, with no manual upload
and no vision cost. Gated by `FIGURE_ATTACH_ENABLED`; the teacher review queue is
the correctness gate.

1. Phase 1 — extraction and catalog (migration 49 after integration renumbering)

   - Before: A question that depended on a diagram had no way to carry one; the
     text-only generator could not surface the source figure.
   - After: During document analysis, captioned figures are extracted
     deterministically from the PDF (PyMuPDF renders the page region above each
     "Figure 8.1 …"/"Fig 3.4" caption, covering vector diagrams, not just raster
     images), stored as PNGs in a private `question-figures` bucket, and catalogued
     in `document_figures` with page, label, caption, and bbox. Extraction is
     best-effort and never blocks analysis; uncaptioned diagrams are intentionally
     skipped in v1. A dev QA script reports per-document caption/figure counts.

2. Phase 2 — attachment and teacher confirmation (migration 50 after integration renumbering)

   - Before: Even with a figure catalog, generated questions had no figure link.
   - After: After a quiz is persisted, a deterministic, decoupled step links each
     figure-dependent question to a catalogued figure of its own source document —
     by explicit label reference ("… in Figure 8.1 …") or by a figure mention plus
     a single figure on the question's source page. It touches none of the
     generation/validation/commit logic (it only issues follow-up updates keyed on
     each question's id) and never raises, so it cannot affect generation. The
     review card shows the attached figure (image, label, caption, source page) and
     lets the teacher confirm (approve as-is), swap to another of the document's
     figures, or remove it. A new `PUT /api/v1/questions/{id}/figure` validates that
     any chosen figure belongs to the question's own document.

Deployment: apply migration 49 then migration 50, set `FIGURE_ATTACH_ENABLED=true`,
and re-run document analysis so figures are catalogued before generation. With the
flag off, no figure rows are written and behaviour is unchanged.
