# 8-Sep-2026

## akbar/insitute_Admin

1.

   - Before: Institution admins could create both teacher and student accounts, assign a course during creation, and delete either role from the Users page and API.
   - After: Institution admins can create and delete teachers only; students continue to join through self-signup, and the backend rejects the former student-management contract.

2.

   - Before: Teachers and students appeared together in a compact user list that did not expose the teaching hierarchy.
   - After: The page and sidebar are teacher-focused, and responsive teacher cards show identity, assigned course count, and assigned subject count with search and pagination.

3.

   - Before: An institution admin could assign teacher subjects but could not drill down from a teacher to their courses and enrolled students.
   - After: Expanding a teacher loads courses derived from explicit subject assignments; expanding a course loads its student count and a scrollable roster with names and emails.

4.

   - Before: No institution-admin endpoints exposed the teacher-to-course-to-student hierarchy.
   - After: Added `GET /api/v1/teachers/{teacher_id}/courses` and `GET /api/v1/courses/{course_id}/students`, with explicit tenant and role filters on every database query and 404 responses for cross-tenant IDs.

5.

   - Before: There were no focused regression tests for teacher-only administration or tenant-isolated hierarchy responses.
   - After: Added tests covering teacher-only lists, student exclusion, accurate student counts, cross-tenant denial, teacher-only create validation, and prevention of student deletion through the management API.

6.

   - Before: Institution admins had to invent temporary passwords manually and could not copy a newly created teacher's email and temporary password from the teacher card.
   - After: Added a secure temporary-password generator and a per-card Copy login action for credentials created during the current page session, without storing plaintext passwords in the database or exposing existing passwords.

7.

   - Before: Course and subject cards used large footer action bars, and opening a course required clicking a separate Subjects button.
   - After: The full course card now opens its subjects, the footer action bars are removed, and compact top-right delete icons on both course and subject cards retain the confirmation step.

8.

   - Before: A row with only one or two course or subject records stretched each card across a wide responsive column, making the cards much longer than the supplied reference.
   - After: Course and subject grids now use compact 280–320px auto-filled columns, matching the reference proportions while still flowing responsively on smaller screens.

9.

   - Before: Course-less teacher creation failed because Supabase applies custom app metadata after the Auth insert trigger, causing the trigger to reject the account before the backend could promote it to teacher.
   - After: Teacher provisioning now uses a validated course from the admin's tenant for safe tenant derivation, promotes the account to teacher, and immediately removes the temporary enrollment so subject assignments remain authoritative.
