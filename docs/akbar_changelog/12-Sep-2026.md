# 12-Sep-2026

## ocr-pavyaa

1.

   - Before: Large-document generation rendered every consolidated topic and then blindly truncated the combined material, so later selected content could be omitted.
   - After: Large-document generation validates teacher-selected topics, renders only their grounded source material, and rejects an unusually oversized selection instead of silently truncating it.

2.

   - Before: A partially processed document could be presented as ready even when one or more chunks had failed.
   - After: Partial jobs block topic selection, show the completed-section count, and provide a resumable retry action that preserves successful chunks.

3.

   - Before: Large-document profile, MAP, and REDUCE AI calls were not all charged against the topic-extraction request limit.
   - After: Every logical analysis call is rate-limited, including the initial profile reservation, each MAP operation, and REDUCE consolidation.

4.

   - Before: Character chunks had no source-page traceability and abandoned in-process jobs could remain running indefinitely.
   - After: PDF extraction records approximate character-to-page spans, chunks/topics expose approximate page ranges, and stale running jobs become retryable failures when polled.

5.

   - Before: Consolidated facts were accepted without any evidence check against their attributed source chunks.
   - After: Consolidation performs a non-blocking deterministic sample check and logs warnings when meaningful fact substrings cannot be found in the source.

## fix/teacher-quiz-correctness

1.

   - Before: Selecting more topics than requested questions silently increased the blueprint total while leaving the per-type counts unchanged, producing contradictory AI instructions.
   - After: The topic picker prevents over-selection and prevents reducing the question total below the selected-topic count, while both API paths and the blueprint builder reject invalid direct requests before consuming AI quota.

2.

   - Before: A structurally valid AI response was persisted even when its total or per-type distribution differed from the teacher's exact blueprint.
   - After: Generated questions are checked against the exact requested distribution before persistence; one corrective generation is attempted on mismatch, and a second mismatch fails without creating a quiz.

## fix/teacher-document-handling

1.

   - Before: Image-only PDFs could upload as successfully completed with a near-empty text preview, then fail later when the teacher tried to analyze them.
   - After: PDF uploads with fewer than 200 readable text characters are immediately marked failed and return clear guidance to use a text-based PDF or the scanned-question-paper Extract Quiz flow.

2.

   - Before: DOCX extraction read only ordinary paragraphs, silently omitting teaching material stored in tables.
   - After: DOCX extraction walks body content in document order and includes non-empty table rows alongside paragraphs.

## fix/grounded-question-regeneration

1.

   - Before: Regenerating one question sent only the existing question and answer to the AI, so the replacement could introduce facts absent from the teacher's document.
   - After: New quiz sets retain their source-document link, large-document jobs retain the exact teacher-selected topics, and regeneration reloads that original selected-topic material with an explicit source-only grounding instruction. Legacy questions without recoverable source material now show an actionable error instead of generating ungrounded content.
