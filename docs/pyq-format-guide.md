# PYQ Upload — PDF Format Guide (for teachers)

To upload Previous Year Questions, prepare your document in the **exact format**
below and export it as a **PDF**. Following this format lets the system read every
question, option, and answer with ~100% accuracy.

> Tip: Type it in **Google Docs** or **Microsoft Word**, then use
> **File → Download / Save as → PDF**. Do **not** upload a scanned or photographed
> PDF — it must be a typed (text) PDF.

---

## The rules (please follow exactly)

1. Every question starts on a new line with **`Q` + number + `.`** — e.g. `Q1.`, `Q2.`
2. Straight after the number you may add an optional **`[PYQ YEAR]`** tag to mark it
   as a previous-year question and record the year — e.g. `Q1. [PYQ 2019]`.
3. Give **exactly four options**, each on its own line, starting with
   **`(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)`, `(D)`** — capital letters, in round brackets.
4. Add **one `Answer:` line** with a single letter — `A`, `B`, `C`, or `D`.
5. (Optional) Add one **`Explanation:`** line — a short reason for the answer.
6. Leave **one blank line** between questions.
7. You do **not** need to write the subject or chapter in the file — you pick those
   from the dropdowns when you upload.

---

## Blank template (copy this)

```
Q1. [PYQ YEAR] Type the full question here. It can run onto
more than one line if it needs to.
(A) First option
(B) Second option
(C) Third option
(D) Fourth option
Answer: <A / B / C / D>
Explanation: (optional) short reason for the correct answer.

Q2. [PYQ YEAR] Next question here?
(A) First option
(B) Second option
(C) Third option
(D) Fourth option
Answer: <A / B / C / D>
```

---

## Filled example (this is what a correct file looks like)

```
Q1. [PYQ 2019] A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple
interest. In how many years will it become four times?
(A) 16 years
(B) 24 years
(C) 32 years
(D) 20 years
Answer: B
Explanation: Doubling in 8 years means interest = principal in 8 years, so 3x
interest (to reach 4x) takes 24 years.

Q2. [PYQ 2021] The mean of the first 10 natural numbers is:
(A) 5
(B) 5.5
(C) 6
(D) 4.5
Answer: B

Q3. The value of 15% of 200 is:
(A) 25
(B) 30
(C) 35
(D) 40
Answer: B
```

---

## What breaks it (avoid these)

- Scanned images or photos of question papers (no readable text).
- Using `1.` `2.` for options instead of `(A) (B) (C) (D)`.
- More or fewer than 4 options.
- Missing the `Answer:` line.
- Two questions on the same line, or no blank line between questions.

---

## Notes

- Q3 above has no `[PYQ ...]` tag — that's fine on the PYQ upload (everything you
  upload here is treated as PYQ); the tag is only there to record the **year**.
- v1 supports 4-option multiple-choice questions. Other types (numerical, true/false)
  can be added later.
